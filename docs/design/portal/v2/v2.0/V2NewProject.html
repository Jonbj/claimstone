News tone and returns

Do financial news items carry information about subsequent stock returns, and under what conditions does any such effect survive? Scope: firm-level news and equity returns; horizons from days to months; text sentiment and event content. Out of scope: macro announcements and crypto assets.

What happens when you start

Claimstone does the reading. You decide at a few marked points.

  1. YFreeze the protocol. Topic and questions become version 1, dated. Later changes are a new version.
  2. CSearch scholarly indexes and citations.
  3. CObtain legal copies. You approve any retry of a refused source, and may upload a copy you hold.
  4. CRead and annotate every passage; each claim is checked against its quote.
  5. YApprove the reading budget before paid model work starts.
  6. CIndependent review by a second model, then one evidence profile per question.
  7. YRead and sign a verdict for each question.
