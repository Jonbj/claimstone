# Second adversarial review — verdict path

Review date: 2026-09-25. Scope: the requested D14–D17, four new specs, prior fixes, and current code. This is a review of the working tree, not of commit messages. Stages 3–6 remain unimplemented in the CLI (`claimstone/cli.py:257-261` and the stage stubs that follow); findings about those stages assess their specs and plans, not running behavior.

## Findings, ranked by consequence

### 1. Critical — the proposed effect verdict is a vote count with a threshold

The `effect` rule declares `SUPPORTED` when three distinct `source_id`s agree and two `sample` strings differ; it records contrary studies but gives them no influence unless they independently meet the same bar (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-verdict-contract-design.md:53-70`; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:64-79`). Three small, imprecise, biased positive results and one large, precise negative result would be labelled `SUPPORTED`. Calling the rule “count and coverage” does not solve the vote-counting problem: the output still turns directions and counts into an evidence-strength conclusion. The records already request estimates, standard errors, horizons, designs and dependence (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage4-extract-design.md:28-39`), but stage 6 discards most of them. D15 had expressly called for risk of bias, missing precision and material counter-evidence (`docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md:369-390`); D17 silently drops that standard (`docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md:327-349`). [Cochrane Handbook chapter 12](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-12) permits direction-of-effect synthesis when pooling is infeasible, while noting that it cannot incorporate magnitude or study size; it is not a warrant for this `SUPPORTED` threshold.

**Change:** keep the study-result table; add prespecified eligibility, outcome/horizon/estimand, design and bias assessment, effect size and uncertainty, dependency/dataset links, and a material-effect threshold for each effect question. Present the effects and intervals, including counter-evidence, even when no pooled number is justified. Use a documented human adjudication of the evidence profile for substantive `SUPPORTED`/`CONTRADICTED`, or rename the automatic output to a strictly descriptive direction count. Do not implement the current automatic effect verdict. A pooled estimate is optional only after compatibility and variance checks; a *magnitude assessment* is not optional for an actionable effect claim.

`min_distinct_data_sources` is especially unsafe as an independence check: two spellings of one dataset pass, while identical broad sample labels can mask distinct datasets (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:76-79`). Unknown linkage must stay unknown pending adjudication. Build explicit canonical dataset/cohort IDs and a dependency graph before counting independent replications.

The heterogeneity rule has a parallel logic error: an effect can differ materially between two nonzero subgroups; an “absent case” is neither necessary nor sufficient to establish moderation (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-verdict-contract-design.md:72-83`; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage4-extract-design.md:42-53`). Extract the actual contrast or interaction estimate, uncertainty and whether it was prespecified. [Cochrane Handbook chapter 10](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-10) distinguishes subgroup contrasts from evidence of no effect within one subgroup.

### 2. Critical — stage 6 can publish a verdict from incomplete or damaged evidence

Stage 6 blocks only `INSUFFICIENT_ACQUISITION` and explicitly allows a final verdict while claims await review (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:49-58,104-111`). One later `SUPPORTED` review can reverse `NEVER_ASKED` or clear the three-source bar. `NEVER_ASKED` is defined as no accepted claim citing a question (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:72-74`), although an all-rejected question is explicitly acknowledged as distinct from a question nobody wrote about (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:104-107`). Extraction misses also look like silence. The “all four lanes completed” denominator helps (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage4-extract-design.md:169-171`) but cannot establish that no relevant passage was missed.

The actual `admit()` can return `OK` on the *obtained* basis while normalization is incomplete (`claimstone/admissibility.py:131-162`). In a temporary store with four found/acquired candidates, one confirmed `NOT_A_DOCUMENT`, and three still awaiting normalization, it returned `OK`, `rate: 1.0`, `confirmed: 0`, `awaiting_normalize: 3`. Thus stage 6's only stated gate is not a final-document gate. More fundamentally, `Store.read()` skips a torn final line and merely records it (`claimstone/store.py:63-84`); `admit()` never checks `torn_tail` (`claimstone/admissibility.py:58-87,156-169`). A lost candidate tail can shrink the denominator and inflate the gate. After a torn line, `Store.append()` writes directly after it (`claimstone/store.py:51-54`), turning a recoverable tail into interior corruption. I reproduced: complete row, torn row, append complete row; the next read raises `LedgerCorrupt`. `repair()` exists but no append path invokes it (`claimstone/store.py:86-102`).

**Change:** separate `PROVISIONAL_OBTAINED` from final admission; require zero awaiting normalization, a complete round, all eligible chunks/lane calls and reviews accounted for, zero unresolved ledger damage, and no unexplained orphan acquisition before final verdicts. Give `NEVER_ASKED` only after explicit question-level screening or call it `NO_VERIFIED_CLAIM`; show rejection and extraction coverage alongside it. On torn tails, block append or repair before append with an audit record, then reconcile missing work. Label any output before these conditions **provisional evidence table**, with no final verdict.

### 3. High — D17 removes the wrong statistical machinery and loses error control

D17 is right that Bonferroni/BH cannot be applied directly without test statistics or p-values (`docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md:340-347`; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:31-43`). It is wrong to infer that count thresholds across five effect questions have no family-level false-positive risk. Illustration only, *not* a measured error rate here: under independent random signs and exactly three studies per null question, a three-positive rule fires with probability 1/8; across five independent null questions, the chance of at least one positive is `1-(7/8)^5 ≈ 0.487`. The actual studies/questions are dependent, selected and heterogeneous, so this arithmetic is a warning, not a calibration. Disclosing the number of questions is transparency, not error control.

There is also a direct document conflict: the verdict contract still promises a computed five-to-ten-question multiplicity family and a correction (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-verdict-contract-design.md:130-140`; `docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md:310-315`), while D17 and stage 6 say no correction (`docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md:340-347`; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:31-47`). The family should be fixed from preregistered questions and intended analyses, not computed from verdicts produced; the latter is outcome-dependent.

**Change:** reconcile these documents before implementation. If the output is descriptive, avoid inferential `SUPPORTED` and state that the five-question family has no controlled error rate. If it makes confirmatory claims, prespecify the family and calibrate decision rules using audited null/negative controls or a defensible inferential model, with dependence and selection addressed. H24 asks whether published effects survive correction (`projects/alembic-s4/questions.yaml:87-90`), which must be assessed per study or analysis family, not satisfied by an endorsement of “good method.”

### 4. High — versioning still permits the meaning of a verdict to drift

The registry hash now includes `id`, `text` and `kind`, a real fix (`claimstone/config.py:249-265`). But verdicts also depend on thresholds and class semantics (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-verdict-contract-design.md:164-185`), the source gate and language policy (`claimstone/config.py:307-342`), and extraction/reviewer choices. None is covered by the registry hash. Stage 6 proposes a `verdict_rules_version`, but the project config has no `verdict_rules` field yet (`claimstone/config.py:82-99`), and stage 6's rule requires a “methodological class” for which the source-class data model has no role field (see `claimstone/config.py:46-55`; `projects/alembic-s4/sources.yaml:14-18`; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:62-69`). The code also records any unseen registry version without requiring a later freeze date or a monotonic version (`claimstone/config.py:267-300`); an unchanged date with v4 was accepted in a temporary-store check.

**Change:** treat the registry digest as question identity only. Version and hash the complete decision contract separately: rule thresholds, source-class roles, eligibility definitions, gate policy, extraction schema/prompt, reviewer model/prompt and aggregation code. Put those identities and round IDs on verdict rows and compare only like with like. Enforce version/date monotonicity for actual registry bumps. Define the methodological role explicitly in `sources.yaml`; do not infer it from a class name.

### 5. High — the new gate policy moves genre coupling into a project-wide switch

The English reference headings are configurable, but the citation regex remains English and fixed (`claimstone/fulltext.py:61-80,146-151`). More seriously, `structural_signal: none` makes any HTML over `min_text_chars` full text (`claimstone/config.py:307-342`; `claimstone/fulltext.py:181-198`). Reclassifying the six real `ABSTRACT_ONLY` HTML artifacts with this setting admitted IND002, IND003, IND004 and IND006 as `HTML_FULLTEXT`; IND005 and NEW002 stayed too short. This is a four-of-six false admission on the available negative examples, not a hypothetical. A mixed scholarly-paper/regulatory-filing corpus cannot choose the right rule per project. `FullText.as_row()` persists only the *count* of paywall phrases, so different same-length lists appear identical in the ledger (`claimstone/fulltext.py:94-110`).

**Change:** make source genre or declared class choose a versioned gate policy; keep a distinct document/summary discriminator for non-citing HTML, calibrated against positive and negative artifacts. Persist the full policy hash and matched policy identity. A user-supplied list is useful configurability, but not sufficient evidence that invariant 4 is satisfied.

### 6. High — the model-call echo fix does not verify a production prompt

The plan fixes backend-level queue collapse and preserves attempts (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-24-model-call.md:660-691`). It adds `RawAnswer.prompt_sent`, but `build_result()` verifies only when that optional field is populated (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-24-model-call.md:752-760,1036-1067`). The CLI, Ollama and local runner return paths shown in the plan do not set it (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-24-model-call.md:1417-1438,1689-1696,1862-1869`); tests set it only in fake runners (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-24-model-call.md:903-926`). As planned, production calls will report `prompt_verified: false`. The `(call_id, backend)` identity also permits a later run with another model under the same backend to appear already answered (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-24-model-call.md:660-691`), and reports can conflate models.

**Change:** make each runner build one exact payload, hash that payload at the transport boundary, and store backend, model and harness identity on every request/result key. Treat a missing prompt receipt as unverified; do not claim the original finding fixed until production runners populate and check it. For chat APIs, hash a canonical serialized message list rather than a reconstructed text echo.

## Assessment of the new stage specs

**Question kinds.** Excluding genuinely local architectural decisions from *literature* verdicts is right, but all 28 registry questions still need an explicit per-question report status. The current “no verdict” for six questions (`projects/alembic-s4/questions.yaml:8-13`; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-verdict-contract-design.md:106-128`) conflicts with the standing “one verdict per question” contract (`README.md:6,33-34`; `CLAUDE.md:9-10`). Say `LITERATURE_VERDICT_NOT_APPLICABLE` or a separate local-validation track; avoid silently omitting rows. H10 is a methodological requirement with an operational application, so `method` is defensible if split into “timestamp bias in literature” and “our pipeline's timestamp is genuinely observable/executable” (`projects/alembic-s4/questions.yaml:44-46`). H13 combines a claim about discovery with a trial-ledger/multiplicity prescription; split it before asking one `method` rule to prove both (`projects/alembic-s4/questions.yaml:53-55`). H23 combines general post-cost effect evidence with “at the size we would trade,” which only local order-size/liquidity data can answer (`projects/alembic-s4/questions.yaml:83-86`). H26 asks whether *the reported relationship* is causally identified, a study-specific design appraisal, not a generic method endorsement (`projects/alembic-s4/questions.yaml:95-98`). H27 combines a proposed mechanism and out-of-sample predictions and needs a contrast/prediction appraisal, not necessarily an absent case (`projects/alembic-s4/questions.yaml:99-101`). H03 may be a heterogeneity/contrast question rather than a scalar effect (`projects/alembic-s4/questions.yaml:23-25`). Keep H04 operational for the S4 mandate, while separating the generic horizon/persistence evidence (`projects/alembic-s4/questions.yaml:26-28`). These are proposed splits, not claims that every existing assignment is false.

**Stage 4.** Check 6 catches an inconsistent model-written numeric value (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage4-extract-design.md:70-101`); it is useful arithmetic validation, though redundant if the engine alone computes and stores normalized values. It does not validate the estimand, unit or whether `(0.008)` is a standard error in the same scale as `2.4%` (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage4-extract-design.md:28-39,70-78`). The proposed numeral regex omits scientific notation and Unicode minus (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage4-extract-design.md:103-109`); exact comparative phrase matching will reject inflected but equivalent forms (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage4-extract-design.md:111-116`). Whole-record rejection is appropriate for an *atomic* result, but the spec does not define result atomicity and says a record is per `(chunk, question)` (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage4-extract-design.md:131-143,173-180`), so multiple estimates for one question in one chunk can be forced into one record or dropped. Specify one record per result/contrast, with an estimate ID and provenance; run a real rejection audit before declaring the cost acceptable.

**Stage 5.** Whole-chunk review and refusal of an identical backend/model are worthwhile controls (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage5-review-design.md:11-37`). But `OVERSTATED` among accepted claims measures a conditional precision-like rate, not extraction quality or recall (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage5-review-design.md:78-90`). A backend that emits one bland safe claim can win while missing ten material results. One fixed reviewer controls reviewer variation but can retain systematic bias and is not ground truth. Compare paired `(chunk, question)` units; report verified useful results, misses, gate rejection and overstatement, with a blind human-adjudicated sample. Record a canonical underlying model identity, since different endpoints need not mean different models.

**Stage 6.** The evidence table is the correct core artifact (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:82-111`). As written, it emphasizes source counts and has no effect magnitude, uncertainty, design quality or dependency links, although those fields are extracted (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage4-extract-design.md:28-39`; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:86-99`). Add a per-result evidence profile with those fields and explicit counterexamples. The example `by_class` appears to count supporting sources only, so it will not fully show the class mix of contrary evidence (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:113-135`). Keep deterministic code for joins, accounting and rendering; remove automatic substantive `SUPPORTED` until its rule is defensible. No R subsystem is required now, but dropping a pooled number must not mean dropping quantitative assessment.

## What to build next

The writing-to-software ratio is now a finding because stages 4–6 have no real output on which to calibrate their thresholds, independence assumptions or false-rejection rate (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage4-extract-design.md:3,182-185`; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage5-review-design.md:3`; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:3`). Stop expanding specs and implement one complete effect question through normalization, extraction, review and an evidence profile, with one negative result and one duplicated dataset. Use those records to revise the rules and measure gate/reviewer errors. In parallel, finish acquisition: the stated floor is 0.80 and the current 15/25 obtained, or 14/25 after excluding the fact sheet, cannot pass (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-stage6-synthesize-design.md:55-58`; `claimstone/admissibility.py:131-162`). Twenty of 25 confirmed documents are needed. Recovering the four paywall cases alone would yield at most 18 confirmed if the present 14 is correct, so resolve the other missing sources too. A small public pilot corpus that actually clears its preregistered floor can validate the vertical slice while this corpus remains gated. Do not lower the floor to make the final verdict path appear usable.
